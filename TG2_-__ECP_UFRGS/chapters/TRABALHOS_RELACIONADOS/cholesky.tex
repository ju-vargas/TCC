The TL-BD-INSA detector of~\cite{wang2021tlbdinsa} implements Cholesky-based inversion
of Hermitian block-diagonal sub-matrices on a Virtex-7 XC7VX980T FPGA
and a TSMC 45~nm ASIC, with 15 bits for each of the real and imaginary parts. The
inversion module uses a triangular systolic array for the Cholesky
factorization, followed by a forward substitution unit, which computes $\mathbf L^{-1}$, and a matrix multiplication unit, which computes $(\mathbf L^{H})^{-1}\mathbf L^{-1}$.
It is the hardware reference closest to the $M_c \times M_c$ inversion of BCD-MMSE, but it considers AWGN and inverts blocks of the Gram matrix. The complete detector, with matched filter, Gram matrix, inversion and estimation, uses 13,640 LUTs, 19,460 FFs and 277 DSP48 at 310~MHz, with a latency of 260 cycles \cite{wang2021tlbdinsa}. These numbers do not \textcolor{GREEN}{report the inversion separately}.
% FONTE: wang2021tlbdinsa L75 (canal com R e T), L485 (Cholesky, multiplicação m_UE x m_UE), L503, L556 (15 bits por dimensão complexa), L582 (m = 4, m_UE = 2), L530-L546.

The BD-NS-EPA detector also inverts block-diagonal sub-matrices by Cholesky, in a 65~nm ASIC, with an average of 11 bits for each of the real and imaginary parts. Each inversion occupies 12.04~kGE of the 2,595~kGE of the detector \cite{wang2023bdnsepa}.

%   L610 (receptor centralizado); Seç. "BD-Matrix Inversion Based on Cholesky" e Alg. 3, linha 7; L48 (ASIC);
%   L86 (canal com R e T), L425 (ordem 2 m_UE), L464 (128 x 32, m = 16, m_UE = 2, kappa = 0,05), L536 (11 bits em média nas partes real e imaginária), Tab. II e L603.

A ZF detector uses a 28~nm ASIC unit that only factors the matrix, and another unit performs the substitution. It uses 13 bits and was sized for 20 million factorizations per second \cite{gangarajaiah2017cholesky}.
% FONTE: gangarajaiah2017cholesky (referencias/Cholesky/A_Cholesky_decomposition_based_..._adaptive_interpolation.md) L8, L17, L29 (AWGN), L170 (13 bits, 20 M CD/s, FBS à parte), Tab. II (L175-L190).

The three detectors are centralized~\cite{wang2021tlbdinsa,wang2023bdnsepa,gangarajaiah2017cholesky}.
