The FD-LAMA detector of~\cite{li2019fdlama} was implemented in HLS on a
Virtex-7 XC7VX690T for $U = 8$ users, 32 antennas per cluster, QPSK and i.i.d. Rayleigh fading. For $C = 1$ cluster, it uses 11,739 LUTs and 219 DSP48 blocks at 429~MHz, with a latency of 310 cycles. Resource utilization scales roughly linearly with $C$,
while the latency rises to 384 cycles at
$C = 4$ ($128 \times 8$)~\cite{li2019fdlama}. The work considers AWGN and does not invert matrices.

The decentralized Newton-method detector of~\cite{kulkarni2021newton}
was implemented on a Virtex-7 XC7VX690T for a $128 \times 8$ system with
$C = 4$ clusters in a ring topology. Its error rate is close to that of centralized ZF. For 16-QAM with $T = 3$ iterations, it uses 111,277 LUTs,
1,824 DSP48 blocks, and 7.177~W of on-chip power at 340~MHz, with a latency of 701 cycles~\cite{kulkarni2021newton}. The work does not define the noise model.
