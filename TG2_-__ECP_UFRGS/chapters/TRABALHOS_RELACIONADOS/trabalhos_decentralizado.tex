\section{Decentralized MIMO}\label{subsec:decentralized-trabs}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
% FONTE: TG1 (sbc-template.tex), Decentralized MIMO Architectures.
The fundamental architecture was established
in~\cite{decentralized2017}, which introduced DBP with the Alternating Direction
Method of Multipliers (ADMM) and Conjugate Gradient (CG) solvers, performing
inversion implicitly within each consensus round. Subsequently, the \textcolor{red}{Partial and Full Decentralization (PD and FD)} architectures were formalized
in~\cite{jeon2019}, demonstrating throughput exceeding 1~Gb/s for a
$256 \times 16$ configuration under perfect channel state information.

\textcolor{red}{Outros trabalhos buscaram reduzir o custo da inversão explícita.} A trade-off analysis between
explicit and implicit Cholesky factorization was provided in~\cite{li-tradeofs},
\textcolor{red}{mostrando que reutilizar a fatoração no bloco de coerência divide por cinco o coeficiente do termo cúbico dominante.} A recursive MMSE chain
with an $M_c \times M_c$ inversion per cluster was proposed in~\cite{zheng2022},
achieving centralized-MMSE equivalence. The Groupwise Recursive
Least Squares (GRLS) algorithm was introduced in~\cite{chen2024grls}.

A parallel line of work pursued inversion-avoidance strategies.
A daisy-chain coordinate-descent algorithm was proposed in~\cite{rodriguezsanchez2020}, \textcolor{red}{e o algoritmo Bayesian Distributed Kaczmarz, em \cite{croisfelt2021}.}
The Groupwise Expectation Propagation (GW-EP) algorithm for both star and
daisy-chain topologies was developed in~\cite{li2023iotj}. \textcolor{red}{O detector BD-NS-EPA (\textit{Block-Diagonal Neumann-Series Expectation Propagation Approximation}) é centralizado, inverte submatrizes bloco-diagonais por Cholesky e tem uma implementação em ASIC (\textit{Application-Specific Integrated Circuit}) \cite{wang2023bdnsepa}.}

Most recently, spatial correlation and imperfect CSI were addressed in~\cite{zhuang2025}.

All the articles cited above assume a diagonal noise covariance matrix.

\textcolor{red}{Até onde se sabe, só dois trabalhos} simultaneously address colored noise and
decentralized LMMSE equalization ~\cite{zhao2021-globecom,zhao2025jsac}. The BCD-MMSE algorithm for daisy-architecture introduced in ~\cite{zhao2021-globecom} requires a local $M_c \times M_c$ Hermitian matrix inversion
at each DU. The framework was extended in ~\cite{zhao2025jsac}, with the introduction of the sDR-MMSE and cDR-MMSE algorithms alongside BCD-MMSE. \textcolor{red}{Esse trabalho mostra que o BCD-MMSE chega perto da SER do LMMSE centralizado \cite{zhao2025jsac}.}
